our august

Five nights. One very good month. Here’s everything I’ve got planned for us.

  1. Aug 1 Saturday

    ♡ Dinner at Gemini

    7:30 PM · 1115 Pearl St

    A table for two on Pearl to start the month off right. Just us, a long dinner, and nowhere else to be.

  2. Aug 8 Saturday

    ♡ Picnic at Lost Gulch Overlook

    Sunset · Flagstaff Rd

    I’m packing dinner and a blanket. We drive up Flagstaff, find a rock, and watch the sun go down over the foothills — it dips around 8:05.

  3. Aug 11 Tuesday

    ♡ To Use a Mountain

    7:00 PM · Boedecker Cinema

    Opening night. Doors are at 6 with a catered reception, so come hungry — and the director sticks around afterward for a conversation with the audience. 99 minutes, and apparently a gorgeous one.

  4. Aug 21 Friday

    ♡ Tuscan Summer Night

    7:00 PM · Sur La Table, Boulder

    A hands-on cooking class — steak, caprese, something lemony for dessert. We cook it, then we eat it. I make no promises about my knife skills.

  5. Aug 29 Saturday

    ♡ 5000 Eyes

    7:00 PM · Fiske Planetarium

    We lean back under the dome and let them map the whole universe at us. A fitting way to close out the month — very big sky, very small us.

♡

Happy anniversary.

— Dawson
